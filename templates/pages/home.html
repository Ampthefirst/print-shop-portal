{# ---- The differentiator ---- #}

Anyone can print a file.

The hard part is deciding what the file should be.

Plenty of shops will take an .stl you already have and run it through a printer. That is a printing service, and it is genuinely useful — we offer it too.

But most people who need a custom part do not have a file. They have a broken bracket, an awkward gap on a shelf, a jig they have been meaning to build for two years, or an idea sketched on the back of an invoice. Turning that into something manufacturable is design work, and it is the part we actually specialise in.

You describe the problem and the constraints. We model the part in CAD, talk it through with you, revise it until it is right, and then manufacture it. You end up with a working part and the files, which are yours to keep.

Every order runs through your account, so quotes, files, messages and payment history stay in one place instead of scattered across an email thread.
