01 · The core service

Fully custom design and manufacturing

For when the thing you need does not exist yet.

This is design work first and printing second. You come with a problem — a part that broke and is no longer sold, a fixture that needs to hold something at an exact angle, an enclosure for electronics, a prototype you want to hold before committing to a production run. There is no file yet, and that is the point.

We work out the requirements with you: what it has to fit, what loads it takes, what it has to survive, how it should look. Then it gets modelled in CAD, shared with you for review, and revised until it is right — before anything is printed and before you have paid for a part that is not quite what you meant.

How the conversation works

Custom work needs back and forth. Every order has its own message thread, and you can request a video call whenever a drawing would be quicker than a paragraph.

You keep the files

Finished designs are delivered as .stl and .step files in your account. Print them elsewhere later, or send them back to us for another run.

On pricing custom work

Design work is quoted after we understand the scope, because a bracket and a multi-part assembly are not the same job. You will always see a price and approve it before work starts. If the scope changes along the way, you get a revised quote and approve that too — there are no surprise charges.

02

Custom branded products

Your identity as a physical object.

Logos, wordmarks and artwork translated into something people can pick up and put on a desk. Useful when a business wants pieces that feel deliberate rather than ordered from a catalogue with a logo slapped on.

Bring whatever you have — a vector logo, a photo of your signage, or a rough sketch. Part of the work is adapting a flat design into something that reads well in three dimensions, prints cleanly, and survives handling.

  • Desk nameplates and office signage
  • Branded gifts for clients and staff
  • Awards, trophies and recognition pieces
  • Event pieces, displays and props

Branded work follows the same route as custom design: quote, design review, then manufacture.
